# 👋 Start here

**From:** Maya Okafor, Product Manager, Dumpling Dash
**To:** Our new developer (that's you)

Welcome aboard. I'm really glad you're here.

Quick background. Dumpling Dash is one food truck, one very talented chef, and one website. Lin Chen started the truck four years ago and her dumplings have a line around the block most days. Last summer we hired Jordan Reyes to build an online ordering site so people could skip the line. Jordan built it fast, it mostly works, and then Jordan left for a startup three weeks ago.

So now it's yours. And honestly? Customers are starting to notice the cracks.

## The goal

The **Harbor Street Food Festival** is coming up, and Lin wants to advertise online ordering on a giant banner. Before we do that, the site needs to be something we're proud of. Your job is to work through the backlog and ship **v1.0**.

## The backlog

Every ticket is in the `tickets/` folder, numbered in the order I'd like you to tackle them. Each one has:

- **What happened**, usually straight from an unhappy customer or from Lin
- **What we know**, which is whatever I managed to find out
- **Done when**, so you know when to stop

The early tickets are about getting your bearings. The middle ones are where customers are hurting. The last one is launch day.
