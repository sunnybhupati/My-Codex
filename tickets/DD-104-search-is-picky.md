# DD-104 · "Searching 'pork' shows one dish"

**From:** Lin, forwarded by Maya · **Priority:** Medium · **Suggested surface:** Codex desktop app

## What happened

> We sell three pork dishes! I typed pork into the search box on my phone and it showed me ONE. Customers will think we don't have them.
>
> — Lin

## What we know

- I tried it. Typing `pork` finds one dish. Typing `Pork` finds two. Neither is right.
- The search box is the first thing customers see.

## Done when

- [ ] Searching `pork`, `PORK` or `Pork` shows every pork dish
- [ ] Searching by a tag like `spicy` or `vegetarian` also works
- [ ] Searching for something silly like `pizza` still shows the friendly "no dishes match" message

This is a good warm-up. Have Codex show you the change before it makes it, and check the result in the browser yourself.
